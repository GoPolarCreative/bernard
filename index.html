What We Do

Electrical Services Across Melbourne

One team for the small repair and the full new build package. Residential, commercial and industrial, all to Australian Standards and all certified.

New Home Electrical Packages

Full electrical package for new homes, rough in through to fit off, coordinated with your builder's program.

Multi Dwelling & Townhouses

Electrical for townhouse and multi dwelling developments, metering, sub mains and unit distribution boards.

Full House Rewiring

Complete rewires for older Melbourne homes, staged so you keep power on where you can while the work runs.

Switchboard Upgrades

Old fuse boards replaced with compliant switchboards, safety switches on every circuit, including three phase.

EV Charger Installation

Home and workplace EV charger installs, with the supply check and circuit upgrades the job actually needs.

Commercial Fit Outs

Office and retail fit outs, lighting, data rough in, power layouts and shopfront works, scheduled around trade.

Three Phase Power

Three phase supply upgrades and installation for homes with heavy loads, workshops and commercial premises.

Industrial Switchboards

Industrial switchboard installation and changeovers, built and tested for the plant it has to run.

Emergency & Exit Lighting

Emergency and exit lighting systems installed, tested and logged so your building meets its obligations.

Maintenance & Safety Checks

Routine maintenance, fault finding and electrical safety inspections for homes, rentals and commercial sites.

We also handle solar installations, smart home automation, safety switch upgrades, lighting design and general fault finding. If it runs on power, ask us.
