What The Package Covers

Everything Between Bare Frame And Handover

A new home package is not a list of power points. It is a sequence, and every stage has to land in the right week or the trade behind you stops. This is what a Bernard Electrics package includes as standard.

01

Temporary builder's supply

Site power and safety switches set up so the frame, the chippies and the concrete crew have compliant power from day one.

02

Consumer mains and metering

Underground or overhead mains run in and terminated, meter box positioned and prepared, ready for the distributor.

03

Full rough in to plan

Every circuit run through frame before the plasterer arrives. Lighting, power, dedicated appliance circuits, exhaust fans and heating and cooling supplies.

04

Switchboard, single or three phase

A properly sized board with RCD protection on the circuits the Wiring Rules require it on, plus spare ways so the house can grow later.

05

Data, TV and comms rough in

Cat6 and TV runs pulled at the same time as the power, which is the only cheap time to do it.

06

Fit off and commissioning

Points, switches, lights, fans, oven and cooktop connections, exhausts and smoke alarms all fitted, labelled and tested.

07

Testing and certification

Every circuit tested and a Certificate of Electrical Safety issued for the prescribed work, which is what the building surveyor and your lender will want to see.

08

Defects and handover

One walk through with you or the builder, and anything on the list closed out before we call it finished.

Fit off is the part people see. Rough in is the part that decides whether the house works.

Scope

Standard Versus The Extras People Ask For

The base package covers a compliant, working house. The second column is the work that is far cheaper now, while the walls are open, than it will ever be again.

Included as standard

  • Lighting and power circuits throughout, run to the electrical plan
  • Dedicated circuits for oven, cooktop, dishwasher and laundry
  • Hardwired, interconnected smoke alarms
  • Exhaust fans and heating and cooling supplies
  • Switchboard with RCD protection and labelled circuits
  • External lighting and weatherproof outdoor points
  • Full test, commissioning and Certificate of Electrical Safety

Worth doing while the walls are open

  • EV charger circuit run to the garage, even if the charger comes later
  • Three phase supply if you have a pool, workshop or heavy heating and cooling load
  • Alfresco, pool and landscape circuits stubbed out to the right positions
  • Cat6 data to every bedroom, the study and the TV wall
  • Smart home automation wiring and switch positions
  • Solar ready conduit from the roof space to the switchboard
  • Extra spare ways in the board so the next change is not a board swap
